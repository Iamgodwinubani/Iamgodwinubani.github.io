Korvanta

Korvanta

Cross-Border Trade Infrastructure

Secure trade infrastructure connecting African businesses with verified suppliers in China, simplifying payments and logistics along the way.

The difference between people who make money importing and people who lose money importing is almost never access to better suppliers. It is whether they paid a little, early, to find out the truth — or a lot, late. Korvanta is built around that.

  • Verified supplier sourcing
  • Inspection before balance payment
  • Payments and logistics in one flow

Vokra Solar

Renewable Energy for Africa

Affordable, reliable solar energy for African homes, businesses and communities — panels, inverters, GEL and lithium batteries, off-grid and hybrid systems, street lighting and solar refrigeration.

Panels are a commodity and the hardware is solved. The constraint is everything around it: financing that matches how people actually earn, batteries specified to survive heat and neglect, and a technician whose income depends on the system still running next year.

  • Home and business solar systems
  • LiFePO4 and deep-cycle GEL storage
  • Off-grid and grid-tied installations

SafePrompt

AI · Security

Detects vulnerabilities in prompts, scores the risk and generates safer alternatives.

Prompt injection is the same class of bug as SQL injection: instructions and data share one channel, and the model cannot reliably tell them apart. SafePrompt works on the assumption that you cannot filter your way out of that — you reduce the blast radius instead.

  • Vulnerability detection and risk scoring
  • Safer prompt generation
  • Enterprise hardening mode
