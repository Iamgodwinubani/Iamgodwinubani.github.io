Overview

Auvetra is a digital trust product built to help people and businesses make more informed decisions in environments where not everything is what it appears to be.

It focuses on detecting signs of scams, verifying information, and presenting risk in a way that is clear enough to act on.

What it focuses on

  1. 01

    Digital trust

    Helping businesses establish confidence in the information and parties they deal with.

  2. 02

    Fraud detection

    Identifying signals that suggest a scam or fraudulent activity.

  3. 03

    Information verification

    Checking information so decisions are based on what can be confirmed.

  4. 04

    Risk awareness

    Presenting risk clearly, so the people making decisions understand what they are looking at.
