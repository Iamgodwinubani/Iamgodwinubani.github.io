Book

Shield Up: Defending Your Digital World

By Godwin Ubani

The complete Gen Z guide to online safety, digital identity and thriving in a cyber world.

Most security advice is written for people who already agree with it. This book is written for everyone else — the people who know they should use a password manager and haven’t, and who have never been given a reason to believe their own actions change the outcome.

  • Threat modelling for people who are not engineers
  • Why fear-based security advice backfires
  • The three controls that matter more than the other twenty

Ventures

Korvanta

The Import Super-App

The super-app connecting Nigerian importers directly to verified Chinese factories. Import smarter, not harder.

Hold a renminbi balance, convert at a live rate, pay suppliers, and talk to a factory in Guangzhou or Yiwu in English while they read it in Chinese. Quotes, negotiation and escrow all sit in the same thread as the conversation.

  • RMB wallet with live ¥ to ₦ conversion
  • Verified factories, not listings
  • Auto-translating supplier chat, EN ⇄ 中文
  • Quotes, negotiation and order tracking
  • Escrow — the balance releases when you say so
