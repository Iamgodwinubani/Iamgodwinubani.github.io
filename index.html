I build technology that closes the distance between ideas and execution, businesses and markets, people and opportunity. My work speaks louder than words—explore the results.

My journey into technology began at Mobile Tech Hub, where I first saw software solve a real problem for a real person. Curiosity turned into obsession quickly — WordPress, HTML, CSS and JavaScript — and the first websites I ever shipped became the foundation of everything that followed.

Wanting to understand how systems actually behave beneath the interface, I moved into computer networking at Aptech and built real depth in network architecture, security protocols and system infrastructure. Today I study Computer Science and Technology at Jiangxi University of Science and Technology in China, learning inside one of the most demanding technology ecosystems in the world.

Beyond engineering, I build companies. I founded Korvanta to make cross-border trade between African businesses and verified Chinese suppliers secure and simple, and Vokra Solar to put affordable, reliable clean energy into African homes and businesses. The mission never changes: build useful things, solve real problems, and create lasting value.
