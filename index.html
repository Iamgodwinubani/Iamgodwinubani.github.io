Who I am and why I build

Godwin Ubani is a Fractional CTO, Software Engineer, and entrepreneur based in China. He builds AI-powered products, provides senior tech leadership, and creates cross-border trade infrastructure for businesses operating between Africa and Asia.

His journey began at Mobile Tech Hub, where he discovered the power of software to solve real problems. He later deepened his technical foundation at Aptech (computer networking) and is currently completing his B.Sc. in Computer Science at Jiangxi University of Science and Technology.

My Mission & Ventures

As founder of Korvanta, Godwin is building secure trade infrastructure to simplify payments between Africa and China, starting with Nigeria. His work spans full-stack development, AI integration, and strategic technology advisory for early-stage companies around the world.

Mission: Build technology that bridges gaps between ideas and execution, markets and opportunity, people and possibility. And building tech software to simplify and give access of digital literacy AI-guided tech education for young Africans.

Beyond the Code

When not leading tech teams or building Korvanta, Godwin creates content for his YouTube channel, documenting his building process, sharing tutorials, and discussing the latest in AI and app development.

He is also the author of Shield Up: Defending Your Digital World, a book aimed at helping individuals and businesses protect their digital presence in an increasingly connected world.

If you want to collaborate, ask a question, or just say hi, feel free to reach out on any of my social platforms. Let's build something great together.
