Shield Up: Defending Your Digital World

By: Godwin Ubani

The complete Gen Z guide to online safety, digital identity and thriving in a cyber world.

Most security advice is written for people who already agree with it. This book is written for everyone else — the people who know they should use a password manager and haven’t, who assume being breached is inevitable, and who have never been given a reason to believe their own actions change the outcome.

It starts with the scenario rather than the principle: what actually happens when someone gets into your email at 2am, and the single moment where it could have gone differently. Then it names the small number of things that carry the weight, and stops talking.
